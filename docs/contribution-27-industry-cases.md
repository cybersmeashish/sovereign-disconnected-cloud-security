# 27 — Three industry case studies

Detailed synthetic cases cover [Government](industry-cases/government.md), [BFSI](industry-cases/bfsi.md) and [Oil & Gas](industry-cases/oil-gas.md). Each names the local decision, data boundary, authority, import/export gates, measured reference outcomes, open deployment questions and link to its corresponding sector repository.

The expanded run allows each site's review acknowledgment with two local approvers and denies unrelated site/tenant access. Government records represent service-review authority, BFSI records represent transaction-review decisions, and Oil & Gas records represent maintenance-review authority. No real public-service determination, funds transfer or industrial-control command is executed.
