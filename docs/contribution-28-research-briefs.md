# 28 — Research results and executive decision briefs

[Original research results](../research/2026-11-18-results.md) publish methods, raw measurements, observed denials, unsafe gaps and reproducibility commands. The [executive decision brief](../briefs/ciso-decision-brief.md) connects a concrete investment decision to operational consequences, measured evidence, uncertainty and the next validation gate.

These are original reference experiments and a decision aid. They have not been peer-reviewed, accepted by a research conference, validated on a provider/customer environment or used to establish a percentile ranking. Preserve the distinction between measured results, declared models and recommendations when sharing.
