# Primary references — contributions 08–28

- [SLSA v1.0 artifact verification](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.0/verifying-artifacts): trust roots, subject digest and expected builder/parameters. No SLSA level claimed.
- [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final) and [official OSCAL catalog](https://github.com/usnistgov/oscal-content): selected control identifiers/titles, assessment aid only.
- [NIST SP 1800-19 supplied preliminary draft](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1800/19/iprd): historical trusted-cloud research; its obsolete-draft status is recorded.
- [AWS Outposts disconnection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/data-residency-hybrid-cloud-services-lens/drhcrel05-bp04.html), [GDC security](https://docs.cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud/hosted/docs/latest/gdcag/security) and [GDC operations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud/hosted/docs/latest/gdcag/security-operations): documentation comparisons, no product tests.
- [NIST FIPS 203](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final) and [FIPS 204](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final): key-establishment and signature transition references.
- [OpenSSL 3.6 pkeyutl](https://docs.openssl.org/3.6/man1/openssl-pkeyutl/): observed maintained CLI compatibility path.
- [PyCA cryptography](https://cryptography.io/): maintained Ed25519/AES-GCM implementation; exact package/wheel inputs pinned in the lock.
- [GitHub advisory API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/security-advisories/global-advisories): exact-version reviewed-catalog snapshots, not vulnerability absence.
